Ziloo



Ziloo is an independent device that sees and senses the environment. It recognises objects and behavior based on what it has been taught. It outputs an event log describing what is perceived.

The bridge board connects the MCU to camera modules. The board gets network connectivity and power over two USB-C connectors.
Two M.2 connectors enables extension boards (Key B & E)
Other connections are used during development for LVDS touchscreen and GPIOs.
