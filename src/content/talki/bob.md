Bob

Computers of the future will not wait for you to tell them what to do. They will take the initiative based on what goes on around them

We’ve created Bob to explore the future of smart products. What if you used digital technology to make something other than a computer? Computers are when you boil it down a type of calculator. A user will instruct it to carry out a task, and it will work on it until done. If nobody instructs it, it will do nothing. It is a tool, just like a bicycle.

This isn’t how nature works. Natural beings are born with goals, and they will work towards them without any instruction. So what if you use technology in the same way. We now have lots of very compact and affordable technology that can be combined in interesting ways.

As clever as your smartphone is, it will only do something if you decide to do it. It is still just a tool. However we can use some of the technology to create a digital being. Step number one is to remove the screen. If there is no user any screen we use shouldn’t be built in. You might connect one for testing and development purposes, but it is only temporary.

So bob is a digital being that is always on. Bob has the hardware to perceive the world. Our challenge is to program Bob to perceive the world and perhaps also react to it.

So why is iBob always on? Have you ever gone up to a dog and looked for the off switch?
We expect electronics to have an off switch, because we use them as tools, but Bob isn’t a tool, so he will also be able to react when you are not around to instruct him.
This of course means that we will have to figure out modes of attention, wakefulness and goal driven behavior.

Bob is made around a modern ARM CPU with dedicated Machine Learning hardware. We have two 1080p camera modules suited for low light. Bob has many more features, but these are the important choices we concluded after trying to turn a raspberry pi into a digital being.

What can you do with Bob? Right now it”s a bit limited since we”re working on the firmware. You can download the alpha version and try out the cool cameras. When the firmware is ready you will be able to run a machine vision model battery driven. You will also be able to teach Bob when to pay attention, and when not to.

Our Roadmap, well roadmaps suck. How do we know when things will work as they should, but here are some things we plan to work on:

- Directional listening and recognition 
- Reliable human detection
- Location sense



### Getting started with Bob

When you first get Bob home, he will get going once connected to a computer or Smartphone.

1. Connect the cable in the box from Bob to a USB-C socket on your Computer or Phone
2. Go to the personal website for your Bob to control it. Either bob.local or boblocal.net.

Your new friend is called Harry Bob Five Ducks. Its a unique name only your Bob has. 


If you want to get started early with Bob, you can get one now with a base Linux running. The full firmware will be released later in the year. With the current software it takes a bit more effort to load TensorFlow and PyTorch models on your new device, but all the fundamental functionality is available 




## Alt

Bob

Computers have gotten really powerful these days, haven’t they? They can do so many things that it things continue, one day soon it will take more than a lifetime to learn about all the things a computer can do for you.
So why do we make computers that we are supposed to use like we would use a hairdryer or a dishwasher. Anyone can become an expert dishwasher operator. They really are just a tool for saving us time washing up.
Computers are different. Many things that we can do with computers, cannot be done without them.
I think we can make computers that work different from the ones we have today. Today you have to tell the computer exactly what you want to do. It does it, assuming that it understood you, and wait for the next thing you want it to do.
But what if you are not there? It won’t be doing anything. That’s quite dumb!
Why can’t it just keep an eye on things while you are gone at least? Surely just that must be useful.

About now some people will say, hey that’s what we are building robots for, we’re working on it. Ok, perhaps, but there are plenty of cool things that don’t require a machine that can move around your house or move other things around.
Let’s say I make a computer that is always on, it can recogise your Pet and what it is up to when you are not there. Hmm, that would be interesting. I wonder if someone has already built that!


This kind of thinking made me do a lot of experiments. The first thing you’d probably do is hook up a WebCamera to Google cloud and try to teach Google to recognise your Pet. But… The more I thought about it the more it felt Eeeew…. I’m not trying to create a free and unedited reality TV channel on the Internet.

So jumping ahead several steps, I made a digital being that can see what is going on. 
I will be able to train it to recognise my Pet all by itself, and perhaps tell it to stop if it pees on the floor.


Why get a Bob?

There are two reasons

1. You want learn and experiment with machine intelligence out in the real world, and you don’t want to become a mobile phone engineer first. We use smartphone technology, but in a different way.
2. You want to explore what computers of tomorrow will be like. Always running and looking to useful things for you before you know that you need it

Bob is meant for the Explorers that look for what we haven’t figured out yet.
