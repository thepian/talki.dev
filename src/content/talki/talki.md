Talki: Language Acquisition in Children

As part of an Interaction Design Thesis for a SUPSI Masters Degree, we created a children’s toy for enriching play and learning. We added modern technology to a traditional doll.

￼

Talki seems to immediately attract the attention of small children, so it might be used for child behavioural research where directing the subject is a challenge.

Talki Skills:
- Identifies Faces
- Tracks Hands
- Identifies Gestures
- Identifies Known Objects
- Identifies Spoken Sounds

This can be used to
- Track language acquisition in specific children
- Track playtime and patterns
- Track what toys a child plays with
- Track vocalisation/speech in specific children
