aboutProject:
  leftCol: >
    <p>BASPIA is taking an honest aim to help Asian singles find a partner. The service aims to align
    incentives by offering value before and after establishing a relationship. BASPIA helps you to
    present your best sides by verifying and highlighting how you have great potential as a mate.
    It helps you to create stimulating moments(dates) with you and your partner.
    The original branding was from 2011 and needed an upgrade. Additionally they wanted to create a mobile App.
    </p>
  rightCol: >
    <p>BASPIA presents a life desired by aspiring asians reflecting a glamorous lifestyle. It is visually
    founded in Fashion as seen in Heritage Brands and Fashion media. It spices the tradition with
    a regular curiosity with experimental and rebel instincts. The base scheme is based on
    monochrome with a use of colours in the photos used. Personal profiles are themed using
    predefined themes that adjusts the feel through spacing and color.
        </p>
