aboutProject:
  leftCol: >
    <p>From 2015–2019 I worked on Swiss telco Sales and Self‑Service applications: Angular WebApps for SME and retail customers backed by an OpenShift/Oracle micro‑services platform. The scope covered Software‑Defined networking for SMEs (order, configure, change) and retail sales & ordering tools used by customers and internal sales channels.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Angular front‑end suite (multi‑app) for sales journeys and self‑service</li>
      <li>OpenShift‑hosted micro‑services with Oracle middleware/DB integrations</li>
      <li>CRM, provisioning and billing integration; SSO and role‑based access</li>
    </ul>
  rightCol: >
    <p>The programmes ran in a regulated, high‑availability environment with zero‑downtime objectives. We delivered incrementally: feature‑flagged rollouts, strong API contracts and close alignment with design system guidelines.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Incremental delivery with canary/staged releases and rollback paths</li>
      <li>API gateway integration and schema‑validated service contracts</li>
      <li>Design system compliant components and accessible flows</li>
      <li>Monitoring, tracing and meaningful product telemetry</li>
    </ul>
whatWeDid:
  leftCol: >
    <p>I contributed across the sales funnel and self‑service lifecycle: from product selection and eligibility to ordering, provisioning and post‑sales changes for Software‑Defined SME networking.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Sales & Eligibility:</strong> Guided sales flows, address checks, options/pricing, quote creation</li>
